Kuna Flow privacy policy

Effective: November 8, 2026

Kuna Flow is a movie player for Apple TV that plays movies from your own Jellyfin media server. We don't collect, sell or share any information about you. There are no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your Apple TV

Your settings, Scenes (bookmarked moments), Kids Mode choices, poster cache and the address of your server are kept on your Apple TV. Your Jellyfin sign-in token is kept in the Apple TV's Keychain. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What goes to your Jellyfin server

Kuna Flow talks to the Jellyfin server you connect it to, which you or someone you trust runs. It sends what any Jellyfin app sends: your sign-in, what you're watching and how far you got (so you can resume), movies you mark as watched, and changes you make to a movie's details. If you turn on Problem notes (Studio → Playback; it's off unless you do), Kuna Flow also writes short technical notes about playback, such as which movie started and why one failed, to your server's own log. None of this reaches us.

Finding your server

To find a Jellyfin server on your home network, Kuna Flow looks on your local network only, after you allow it. The Apple TV asks for permission first.

iCloud

If you use iCloud, your Scenes sync between your own Apple TVs through your iCloud account. Apple handles that sync; we can't see it.

Demo Mode

The demo films stream from the Internet Archive (archive.org) and the Blender Foundation (download.blender.org). Like any website, those servers see your network address when a film plays.

Tips

The optional tip jar uses Apple's in-app purchases. Apple handles the payment; we don't receive your payment details.

Children

Kuna Flow collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
